\begin{abstract}
Precomputed Earth-observation (EO) embedding products promise
plug-and-play features for geospatial prediction, but their relative
merits for demanding, globally distributed tasks remain unclear. We
present the first large-scale comparison of three embedding products
--- TESSERA v1.1, Google AlphaEarth, and Embedded Seamless Data ---
on 17-class Local Climate Zone (LCZ) classification over the So2Sat
LCZ42 \emph{culture-10} benchmark: 400{,}673 patches, 42 training
cities, and 10 held-out evaluation cities spanning all inhabited
continents. Under an identical training recipe, TESSERA leads by
$\sim$0.10 Cohen's kappa (0.619 vs.\ 0.520/0.513), and the kNN and
linear probes agree. Intrinsic representation diagnostics do \emph{not} predict
this ranking: TESSERA is the most geography-entangled embedding by
silhouette and domain-classifier analysis yet wins downstream, and
the one probe that follows the diagnostics --- a generative GMM
density on pooled features, where Seamless leads --- is also the one
that discards spatial patch structure. The
embeddings are strongly complementary --- cross-embedding ensembling
gains +2--4 kappa points at every stage of our campaign, and much of
the optimal (lopsided) ensemble weighting is implicit calibration.
Because validation and test share the same 10 cities, learned
combiners silently exploit city identity: a stacked
logistic-regression combiner scores 0.776 fit on validation but
collapses to 0.612 under leave-one-city-out (LOCO) refitting,
below plain averaging. We therefore report all combiners under a LOCO
protocol. Finally, 13 structural scalars (GHSL built height/surface,
canopy height, OSM land use) alone beat an entire embedding model
LOCO (0.579 vs.\ 0.506), and a confidence-gated offset
corrector using them lifts the honest benchmark state of the art to
kappa 0.706--0.708 (OA 0.732--0.734) --- far above the best
published culture-10 results. We release code, per-city results, and
a catalogue of documented negative results (capacity scaling,
test-time adaptation, stacking, redundant-embedding fusion).
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}\label{sec:intro}

Local Climate Zones \citep{stewart2012lcz} provide a
culture-neutral typology of urban form used across urban climatology.
Global LCZ mapping remains hard: the canonical So2Sat LCZ42
benchmark's cross-city (\emph{culture-10}) split has resisted
progress, with published overall accuracies around
0.61 \citep{zhu2020so2sat,qiu2020sen2lcz}.

Meanwhile, a new class of \emph{precomputed EO embedding products}
--- TESSERA \citep{tessera2025}, Google AlphaEarth
\citep{alphaearth2025}, Embedded Seamless Data \todo{cite} --- offers
dense, annual, 10--30\,m feature maps intended to replace raw imagery
in downstream models. Early comparisons are small-scale: five Swiss
cities for LCZ \citep{swiss_lcz2026}, or task suites without LCZ
\citep{bettertogether2026}. Whether these products transfer to unseen
cities globally --- the operationally relevant setting --- is open.

Contributions:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Large-scale honest comparison} of three embedding
    products on culture-10 under one recipe and across probe families
    (Sec.~\ref{sec:results-solo}).
  \item \textbf{Diagnostics--performance disconnect}: intrinsic
    embedding diagnostics (class/geography silhouettes,
    domain-classifier AUC) mispredict the downstream ranking
    (Sec.~\ref{sec:results-diag}).
  \item \textbf{Complementarity and calibration}: cross-embedding
    ensembles dominate all within-model levers; optimal weights
    largely re-derive per-model temperature scaling
    (Sec.~\ref{sec:results-ens}).
  \item \textbf{Honest-evaluation methodology}: a leave-one-city-out
    audit that exposes city-identity leakage in learned combiners on
    shared-city val/test benchmarks (Sec.~\ref{sec:results-ens}).
  \item \textbf{Structural auxiliary data}: 13 zonal scalars beat a
    full embedding model; a confidence-gated corrector sets a new
    honest benchmark SOTA, kappa 0.706--0.708
    (Sec.~\ref{sec:results-aux}).
  \item \textbf{Documented negatives}: capacity, BN-based test-time
    adaptation (with a label-shift diagnosis), stacked combiners, and
    redundant-embedding fusion all fail here (Sec.~\ref{sec:negatives}).
\end{enumerate}

\section{Data and embeddings}\label{sec:data}

\subsection{Benchmark and split}
So2Sat LCZ42 v4 \citep{zhu2020so2sat}: 400{,}673 geolocated
320\,m patches labelled with 17 LCZ classes across 52 cities. We use
the original \emph{culture-10} split: 42 training cities
(352{,}366 patches); validation (24{,}119) and testing
(24{,}188) drawn from the western/eastern halves of 10 held-out
cities on different continents (Guangzhou, Jakarta, Moscow, Mumbai,
Munich, Nairobi, San Jos\'e, Santiago, Sydney, Tehran). Ensemble
tables use the 23{,}878/23{,}858 val/test patches covered
by all three embedding sources (98.6\% of the original test
set). Two properties matter for evaluation honesty
(Sec.~\ref{sec:methods-loco}): training cities are disjoint from
evaluation cities, but \emph{validation and test share the same 10
cities} (median test-patch to nearest-val-patch distance
\uv{2.55}\,km).

\section{Methods}\label{sec:methods}

\subsection{Classifiers and recipe}\label{sec:methods-recipe}
All embeddings train with an identical recipe on 32$\times$32-pixel
patch crops: timm ResNet-34 with a 3$\times$3 stem, batch 256, lr
$5{\times}10^{-4}$ with 3-epoch warmup and cosine decay, weight decay
$10^{-3}$, mixup $\alpha{=}0.4$, label smoothing 0.1,
$\sqrt{\text{inv-freq}}$ class weights, early stopping on validation
kappa, dihedral test-time augmentation. The recipe was tuned on
TESSERA (the eventual winner) --- a conservative bias we quantify
with \todo{note per-embedding sanity sweeps if run}. Probe families
for ranking robustness: cosine-kNN ($k{=}20$) and a BatchNorm+linear
probe on GAP-pooled features, trained identically.

Capacity was tested and rejected: ResNet-101/152 do not beat
ResNet-34 (0.603/0.591 vs.\ 0.619) --- the bottleneck
is representation and shift, not model size.

\subsection{Semi-supervised noisy student}\label{sec:methods-ssl}
For the system-level results we add three noisy-student rounds
\citep{xie2020noisy} on \uv{286{,}207} unlabeled patches sampled
inside the training-tile footprints, weakly labelled by the Demuzere
et al.\ global 100\,m LCZ map \citep{demuzere2022lcz}
(agreement-gated: teacher top-1 = weak label $\wedge$ confidence
$\geq 0.7$). Kappa: 0.619 $\to$ 0.6419 $\to$ 0.6497;
gains saturate as students converge toward the ensemble consensus.
Matching self-teacher rounds for the weaker embeddings confirm SSL
needs a strong teacher: AlphaEarth 0.5195 $\to$ 0.5218 (keep-rate
11.4\%) and Seamless 0.5125 $\to$ 0.5135 (keep-rate 5.1\% --- the
weak teacher rarely agrees with the weak-label map; 48.9\% of the
pool has Seamless coverage).

\subsection{Ensembling, calibration, and the LOCO protocol}
\label{sec:methods-loco}
Softmax averaging (equal or weighted), per-model temperature scaling
fit on validation NLL, and learned stacking are compared. Because
val/test share cities, any combiner fit on validation can encode
city-conditional structure. Our audit: refit the combiner on 9 of the
10 evaluation cities' validation patches, evaluate on the held-out
city's test patches, pool over the 10 folds (LOCO). Base models never
see evaluation cities, so only combiners need this treatment.

\subsection{Confidence-gated structural offset corrector}
\label{sec:methods-aux}
Per LOCO fold: $\text{logits} = \log p_{\text{weighted}} + A \cdot
\text{aux} + b$, applied only where the ensemble max-probability is
below a gate $\tau$; $A \in \mathbb{R}^{17\times 13}$, ridge
$\lambda$, and $\tau$ are selected city-grouped inside the fold's
nine fit cities. Freezing the probability pathway at coefficient~1 is
essential: free logistic regression on $[\log p \oplus \text{aux}]$
collapses under LOCO like the stacker.

\subsection{Intrinsic diagnostics}\label{sec:methods-diag}
On GAP-pooled features (PCA-50): silhouette scores by class /
city / country / continent; kNN label agreement; train-vs-test
domain-classifier AUC; per-class centroid drift. UMAP visualizations
for qualitative structure.

\section{Negative results}\label{sec:negatives}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Capacity}: ResNet-101/152 $\leq$ ResNet-34
    consistently.
  \item \textbf{Per-city BN test-time adaptation}: AdaBN/TENT lose
    4--8 pooled kappa points (LOCO ensemble 0.6871 $\to$
    0.609/0.616; solo TESSERA 0.6497 $\to$ 0.598/0.608) despite
    helping covariate-shifted cities (Tehran \uv{+11.6}); per-city BN statistics absorb the
    city's class mix --- the shift is \emph{label}-shift-shaped, and
    statistics matching mis-corrects it.
  \item \textbf{Stacked combiners}: leakage autopsy above.
  \item \textbf{Redundant-embedding input fusion}: TESSERA$\oplus$
    AlphaEarth channels 0.5974 $<$ TESSERA alone 0.619
    (contrast: orthogonal \emph{physical} aux channels help,
    Sec.~\ref{sec:results-aux}).
  \item \textbf{SSL saturation}: iteration gains +2.3, +0.8,
    then vetoed; students converge toward ensemble consensus and a
    weak teacher gains little from self-training (AlphaEarth +0.2,
    Seamless +0.1 kappa points solo).
    Confidence thresholds must be set relative to teacher calibration
    (a mixup/label-smoothed teacher has a $\sim$\uv{0.9} softmax
    ceiling).
\end{itemize}

\section{Discussion}\label{sec:discussion}
\todo{Draft. Points: (1) embedding choice matters more than any
training lever, but complementarity matters more than choice; (2)
honest evaluation is fragile on shared-city benchmarks --- LOCO

\paragraph{Limitations.} Three seeds for the solo comparison but
single-seed SSL/ensemble results; 2017 embeddings only; TESSERA
v2 not yet globally available over the benchmark at submission time
(coverage 43.5\% of patches as of 2026-07-14); OA/kappa on a
98.6\% coverage subset of the original test set; the shared
recipe was tuned on the winning embedding.

\paragraph{Future work.} Label-shift-aware correction (EM/BBSE);
rare-class supervision via structural-consistency-gated
pseudo-labels; TESSERA v2 once coverage completes; honest stacking
via out-of-fold predictions over training cities.
